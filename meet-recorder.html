How It Works

  • Tab Audio — Opens screen-share picker; choose your Google Meet tab.
  • Microphone — Records your voice directly (no screen share needed).
  • Tab + Mic — Merges both streams for full meeting audio.
  • Upload — Transcribe a saved .mp3 / .wav file instantly.
  • Files are saved as .webm in your browser; click Download to keep them.
  • Transcription requires a valid API key for the chosen provider.
